[center][b]
Ever installed Astral Ascension but found you would need to wait years for a size 9 colony in the first place?
Ever wanted to see those cool multipliers from having a [i]Fractal Ecumenopolis[/i] - whatever that is, because you've never seen it without using console commands?
Ever installed Vulpoids and saw your celebrities produce 0.6% growth on a size 8?

Well, look no more.

Adds a three-tier cloning industry that massively boosts population growth - at a cost to stability.

[color=red]Requires [url=https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21022.0][u]LunaLib[/u][/url].[/color]
[/b][/center]

[center]
[table]
[tr]
[td][hr][center][size=12pt][b]Industry Chain[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Adds a new industry, occupying an industry slot. Unlocked by finishing the bar mission [b]Inhuman Experiments[/b], or known from the start if you turn the mission off in LunaLib.[/li]
[li][b]Clone Vat Experiments[/b] - entry-level, produces some population growth. Upgrades into one of three paths (with Building Menu Overhaul, under Choose Upgrade):
[list]
[li][b]Cloning Industry[/b] - more population growth, then [b]Cloning Megafactory[/b] for a large amount. Tons of upkeep, credits and stability. Be prepared.[/li]
[li][b]Organ Flashcloning[/b] - supplies harvested organs (colony size plus 1) and adds stability.[/li]
[li][b]Marine Sim-Cloning[/b] - puts trained marines into the colony's storage every 30 days, and lets you clone officers at the colony's [b]Cloning facilities[/b] option.[/li]
[/list][/li]
[li]Growth scales with colony size when an Alpha or Omega core is installed, or when a pristine nanoforge is installed.[/li]
[li]All tiers apply a stability penalty that scales with colony size - there are consequences for cutting corners on biology.[/li]
[li]The growth buildings and Marine Sim-Cloning need harvested organs: colony size minus 2. A shortage cuts their output by the same share.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Organ Flashcloning[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Supplies harvested organs (colony size plus 1) and adds 1 stability.[/li]
[li]AI cores, the story point improvement, a Biofactory Embryo (+2 organs, +1 stability) or a Pristine Nanoforge (+3 organs, +1 stability) add more.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Marine Sim-Cloning and officer cloning[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every 30 days puts 10 marines per colony size into the colony's storage, trained to Experienced.[/li]
[li]Cryoarithmetic Engine: Veteran marines on hot worlds, Elite on worlds with extreme heat. Biofactory Embryo: +50% marines.[/li]
[li]Officer cloning: pay credits and 10 harvested organs to copy an officer. The clone keeps the portrait, personality, level and skills, but elite skills become normal ones, and it has the Clone skill: -15% maneuverability and -25% autofire accuracy.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Clone Vat hull mod[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Logistics hull mod, 4/10/25/40 OP, learned from the same mission.[/li]
[li]Restores 10/25/50/100% of the crew lost in a battle, depending on hull size. The vats of every ship in the fleet add up, to at most 100%.[/li]
[li]Built in (S-mod): every day turns 2/4/8/16 food into 1/2/4/8 crew while the fleet has food and room for crew.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]AI Core Effects[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Gamma core[/b] - reduces upkeep. Cloning is expensive; every bit helps.[/li]
[li][b]Beta core[/b] - reduces upkeep and produces crew and marines alongside population growth.[/li]
[li][b]Alpha core[/b] - reduces upkeep, produces crew and marines, and growth now scales with colony size.[/li]
[li][b]Omega core[/b] [i](Astral Ascension)[/i] - all Alpha benefits, plus doubled colony size scaling. Expect extremely large numbers. Go wild.[/li]
[li]Organ Flashcloning: every core reduces upkeep and adds organs; Beta and up add stability too (Omega: +3 organs, +2 stability).[/li]
[li]Marine Sim-Cloning: every core reduces upkeep and adds marines; Alpha and Omega also train them better (Omega: +100% marines).[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Configuration[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Uses [url=https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21022.0]LunaLib[/url] for in-game settings.[/li]
[li]Configurable values, in tabs: base growth per tier, colony size scaling factor, a global multiplier, whether cloning needs the Inhuman Experiments mission, every Organ Flashcloning, Marine Sim-Cloning, officer cloning and Clone Vat number.[/li]
[li]Players without LunaLib will use the built-in defaults.[/li]
[/list]

[spoiler=FAQ]
[b]Is this mod overpowered?[/b]
Definitely yes, until I manage to balance it somehow. And I mean [i]really[/i] overpowered - hitting over a thousand growth points on a size 7 colony is entirely possible right now.

[spoiler=Version log]
0.1.0
Initial Release
0.1.1
Fixed infinite recursion bug. Now playable.
0.2.1
Added support for story point improvement as well as two colony items:
Biofactory embryos and pristine nanoforge.
0.2.2
New gold icon in mod managers (TriOS) and in LunaLib's settings.
0.3.0
New hull mod Clone Vat (logistics, 4/10/25/40 OP): restores 10/25/50/100% of the crew lost in a battle, depending on hull size. The vats of every ship in the fleet add up, to at most 100%.
Clone Vat built in (S-mod): every day turns 2/4/8/16 food into 1/2/4/8 crew while the fleet has food and room for crew.
New bar mission Inhuman Experiments. Beating the fleet at the end unlocks the cloning colony buildings and teaches Clone Vat.
The cloning colony buildings now need that unlock. Saves that already have a cloning building get it automatically.
0.4.0
New LunaLib setting: Unlock through the Inhuman Experiments mission. When off, the cloning colony buildings and the Clone Vat hull mod are known from the start.
Beating the clone fleet now opens a scene of its own after the battle that shows what you found and what you gained.
0.5.0
Cloning colony buildings now need harvested organs: colony size minus 2. A shortage cuts population growth and the crew and marine output by the same share.
The discovery scene and the building descriptions now mention the specialized nanoforge that weaves biological matter.
0.6.0
Clone Vat Experiments can now upgrade into one of three buildings, picked at the colony's new Cloning facilities option: Cloning Industry, Organ Flashcloning or Marine Sim-Cloning.
New building Organ Flashcloning: supplies harvested organs (colony size plus 1) and adds 1 stability. AI cores (Omega too), the story point improvement, a Biofactory Embryo or a Pristine Nanoforge add more of both.
New building Marine Sim-Cloning: puts 10 marines per colony size into the colony's storage every 30 days, trained to Experienced. A Cryoarithmetic Engine trains them to Veteran on hot worlds and Elite on worlds with extreme heat. A Biofactory Embryo, AI cores (Omega too) and the improvement add more marines. Needs harvested organs.
Officer cloning at colonies with Marine Sim-Cloning: pay credits and harvested organs for a copy of an officer with the same portrait, personality and skills, but no elite skills, plus the new Clone skill (less maneuverability, worse autofire aim).
New LunaLib tabs: Organ Flashcloning, Marine Sim-Cloning, Officer Cloning and Clone Vat. Reset to Default now resets every setting.
Rewrote the Clone Vat Experiments and Cloning Industry descriptions.
0.6.1
Fixed: picking Organ Flashcloning or Marine Sim-Cloning as the upgrade built Cloning Industry instead.
Clone Vat Experiments no longer has a fixed upgrade. With Building Menu Overhaul, Choose Upgrade lists all three paths. Without it, the building's menu has an Upgrade entry for each path and a Cancel upgrade entry.
The Cloning facilities option now only appears with Marine Sim-Cloning, for officer cloning.
The clone of you now always captains the largest ship of the clone fleet.
[/spoiler]
