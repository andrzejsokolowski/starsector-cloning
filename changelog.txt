Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-12)
- New LunaLib setting: Unlock through the Inhuman Experiments mission. When off, the cloning colony buildings and the Clone Vat hull mod are known from the start.
- Beating the clone fleet now opens a scene of its own after the battle that shows what you found and what you gained.

Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-11)
- New hull mod Clone Vat (logistics, 4/10/25/40 OP): restores 10/25/50/100 percent of the crew lost in a battle, depending on hull size. The vats of every ship in the fleet add up, to at most 100 percent.
- Clone Vat built in (S-mod): every day turns 2/4/8/16 food into 1/2/4/8 crew while the fleet has food and room for crew.
- New bar mission Inhuman Experiments. Beating the fleet at the end unlocks the cloning colony buildings and teaches Clone Vat.
- The cloning colony buildings now need that unlock. Saves that already have a cloning building get it automatically.

Version 0.2.2 (2026-11-10)
- New gold icon in mod managers (TriOS) and in LunaLib's settings.

Version 0.2.1 (2026-03-29)
- Support for all vanilla-like colony actions, each with its own effect: story point improvement, colony items (Biofactory Embryo and Pristine Nanoforge) and AI cores.

Version 0.1.1 (2026-03-29)
- Fixed an infinite recursion bug that crashed the game. Now playable.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-03-29)
- Full AI core support: the industry also produces crew and marines with a core above Beta.

Version 0.0.2 (2026-03-29)
- First release: three levels of the Cloning industry, LunaLib settings for population growth, sprites and descriptions.
